Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) & LSTMs — Practical Guide

난이도 / Difficulty: Intermediate
날짜 / Date: 2026-08-19

내용

RNN과 LSTM은 시계열·문자열·스트리밍 데이터 처리에 특화된 순환 구조로, 실무에서는 음성인식·언어모델·세션 기반 추천 등 낮은 지연과 온라인 추론이 필요한 곳에서 여전히 유효합니다.

핵심 작동 원리는 상태(hidden state)를 시간 steps에 걸쳐 전달해 이전 입력의 정보를 유지하는 것입니다. 일반 RNN은 기울기 소실/폭주 문제가 있어, LSTM은 입력·출력·망각 게이트로 장기 의존성을 제어합니다. 수식으로는 게이트들을 통해 cell state c_t와 hidden h_t를 갱신하며, 배치에서는 pack_padded_sequence로 가변 길이를 처리하고 cudnn 최적화(LSTM)의 장점을 활용합니다. 훈련 시에는 gradient clipping, 가중치 초기화(orthogonal), 혹은 layer normalization/skip connection을 도입해 안정성을 높입니다. 그러나 매우 긴 문맥이나 대규모 병렬 처리에서는 Transformer가 대안입니다.

활용

실제 적용 사례와 코드: Baidu DeepSpeech(CTC+RNNs)·Sutskever 등(Seq2Seq LSTM)·Hidasi 등(세션 기반 추천 RNN). PyTorch 예:

import torch.nn as nn
from torch.nn.utils.rnn import pack_padded_sequence, pad_packed_sequence
lstm = nn.LSTM(input_size=80, hidden_size=512, num_layers=3, batch_first=True, bidirectional=True)
packed = pack_padded_sequence(x, lengths, batch_first=True, enforce_sorted=False)
packed_out, _ = lstm(packed)
out, _ = pad_packed_sequence(packed_out, batch_first=True)
logits = linear(out) # for CTC: log_probs = F.log_softmax(logits, dim=-1)

운영 팁: 입력 길이별로 정렬하지 않고 enforce_sorted=False 사용, 학습 시 clip_grad_norm_(model.parameters(), 5), 프로덕션은 cuDNN LSTM 사용(속도)과 ONNX 변환 후 TensorRT로 임베디드·서빙에서 가속. 논문/오픈소스: Hochreiter&Schmidhuber'97, Sutskever et al. 2014, Hannun et al. (DeepSpeech).

Content

RNNs and LSTMs are sequence models that maintain hidden state across time steps; they remain practical for streaming, low-latency inference, and many time-series tasks in production.

RNNs propagate hidden states through time; vanilla RNNs suffer from vanishing/exploding gradients. LSTMs add input, forget, and output gates to control a cell state, enabling learning of longer-range dependencies. In practice you handle variable-length sequences with pack_padded_sequence / pad_packed_sequence, use bidirectional LSTMs for offline context, and prefer cuDNN-backed LSTM kernels for throughput. Training best practices include gradient clipping, orthogonal initialization, layer normalization or recurrent dropout, and monitoring sequence-wise masks. For very long contexts or massive parallelism, Transformers often outperform RNNs, but RNNs/LSTMs are still favorable for streaming/online scenarios.

Applications

Production & research examples: Baidu DeepSpeech (CTC + RNNs), Sutskever et al. (Seq2Seq LSTM), Hidasi et al. (session-based RNN recommendations). PyTorch snippet:

import torch.nn as nn
from torch.nn.utils.rnn import pack_padded_sequence, pad_packed_sequence
lstm = nn.LSTM(emb_dim, 512, num_layers=2, bidirectional=True, batch_first=True)
packed = pack_padded_sequence(inputs, lengths, batch_first=True, enforce_sorted=False)
packed_out, _ = lstm(packed)
out, _ = pad_packed_sequence(packed_out, batch_first=True)
log_probs = nn.functional.log_softmax(fc(out), dim=-1)
ctc = nn.CTCLoss(blank=0)
loss = ctc(log_probs.transpose(0,1), targets, input_lengths, target_lengths)

Deployment patterns: export to ONNX, use cuDNN LSTM for inference speed, clip gradients during training, prefer stateful inference for streaming ASR, and consider replacing with Transformer/Conformer when batch-parallel throughput and long-range context matter. Key refs: Hochreiter & Schmidhuber '97, Sutskever et al. 2014, Hannun et al. (DeepSpeech).
